## Report Interpretation Guide

### Trend Classifications

- **Increasing** (📈): Mentions grew by >20% from start to end
- **Decreasing** (📉): Mentions declined by >20% from start to end  
- **Stable** (➡️): Mentions varied by <10% with low volatility
- **Volatile** (📊): Mentions fluctuated significantly without clear direction

### Status Indicators

- **Complete**: All 7 days of data successfully collected
- **Partial**: Some days missing due to source failures
- **No data available**: Unable to collect any meaningful data

### Key Metrics

- **Total Mentions**: Sum of all mentions across 7 days
- **Average Daily**: Total mentions divided by 7 days
- **Percentage Change**: Change from day 1 to day 7
- **Standard Deviation**: Measure of daily variation

### Using Reports

1. **JSON**: Import into analysis tools, databases, or custom applications
2. **Text**: Share via email, include in documents, or print
3. **HTML**: View in browser, share via web, or embed in websites
